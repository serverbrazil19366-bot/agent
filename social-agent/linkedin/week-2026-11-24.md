```
3. Submit every form yourself. Check the enquiry actually arrives.
```

```
A big discount
→ Brings bargain hunters
→ Cuts your margin
→ Teaches customers to wait for the next sale

A value offer
→ Protects your price
→ Gives people a reason to buy now
```

## Sun 29 Nov: myth, "Google Ads will fix a slow month"

Poster: `posters/2026-11-29-sakthi.jpg` (Sakthi), used as is. Caption adds the mug line and the leaking-bucket image.

Poster text:
- MYTH vs FACT
- MYTH: **"Google Ads will fix a slow month."**
- FACT: Ads bring more people to your door. They can't fix what happens after.
- Check these 3 before spending more on ads:
- 1. Are enquiries answered within an hour?
- 2. Does your landing page say what you do, where, and the price range?
- 3. Do you know which calls and enquiries came from ads?
- Fix the leak first. Then turn up the tap.

```
"It's a slow month. Let's run Google Ads."

A slow month isn't always an ads problem.

Ads bring more people to your door. They can't fix what happens after they arrive.

Before you spend more on ads, check these 3:

1. Are enquiries answered within an hour?
A missed call or a late WhatsApp reply is a lost customer, ad or no ad.

2. Does your landing page say what you do, where, and roughly what it costs?
If people have to call to find out the basics, many won't.

3. Do you know which calls and enquiries came from ads?
Without tracking, you can't tell if the money worked.

If all 3 are fine, ads can help.
If not, more ads only mean more people walking past a broken door.

Fix the leak first. Then turn up the tap.

What do you usually do in a slow month?

#GoogleAds #DigitalMarketing #SmallBusiness
```

```
I run a digital marketing agency on my own.

Here are 5 things nobody told me before I started:

1. Clients don't buy marketing. They buy peace of mind.
They don't want "SEO" or "ads". They want to stop worrying about where the next customer comes from.

2. The hardest part isn't the work. It's saying no.
To the wrong client, the wrong project, the "small extra change" that takes a whole day.

Most people hire whoever answers first and answers clearly.

None of this was in any course I took.
```

- 1. First reply to every WhatsApp enquiry
- 2. Appointment and payment reminders
- 3. Asking for a Google review after the job
- 4. A website enquiry alert straight to your phone
- 5. Scheduling social media posts

```
Most small businesses think automation means expensive AI tools.

Start smaller. Start with the tasks your team repeats every day.

5 to automate first:

1. The first reply to every WhatsApp enquiry
An instant greeting, so nobody waits without an answer.

2. Appointment and payment reminders
Fewer no-shows, fewer awkward follow-up calls.

3. Asking for a Google review
A message after every completed job, sent the same day.

4. Website enquiry alerts
A new form enquiry goes straight to your phone, not just an inbox nobody checks.

5. Scheduling social media posts
Plan the week in one sitting instead of posting every day by hand.

Most of these can be set up with free or low-cost tools.

Automate the repetitive. Keep the personal.

Which task does your team repeat the most?

#Automation #SmallBusiness #Productivity
```
