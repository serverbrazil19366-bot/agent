## 2 · Tue 8 Dec: 5 WhatsApp replies every business should save

Poster text (WhatsApp chat bubbles):
- COPY · PASTE · SAVE
- **5 WhatsApp replies every business should save**
- 01 · New enquiry: "Hi [Name]! Thanks for reaching out. I'm checking this now and will reply in 10 minutes."

```
Most enquiries aren't lost because of price.
They're lost because the reply came too late.

Here are 5 WhatsApp replies every business should save. Copy, paste, change the [brackets]. 👇

1. New enquiry
"Hi [Name]! Thanks for reaching out. I'm checking this now and will reply in 10 minutes."
→ An instant reply stops them from messaging your competitor.

2. "How much does it cost?"
"It depends on [factor]. Most customers pay between ₹[X] and ₹[Y]. Can I ask 2 quick questions to give you an exact price?"
→ Give a range, then start a conversation.

3. After hours
"Thanks for your message! We're closed now and open at [time]. We'll reply first thing."
→ Silence feels like rejection. A reply doesn't.

4. Quote follow-up
"Hi [Name], just checking if you had any questions about the quote. Happy to help."
→ One follow-up. Friendly, not pushy.

5. After the job
"Thank you for choosing us! If you were happy, a quick Google review would mean a lot: [link]"
→ Ask on the same day, while they're happy.

Save them as Quick Replies in WhatsApp Business: Settings → Business tools → Quick replies. Type "/" and send in one tap.

Save this post. Which reply does your business need most? 👇

#WhatsAppBusiness #SmallBusiness #CustomerExperience

## 5 · Fri 18 Dec: Myth, "SEO is dead because of AI"

Poster text:
- MYTH vs FACT
- MYTH: **"SEO is dead because of AI."**
- FACT: AI answers are built from websites, reviews and listings. Clear, accurate content matters more now, not less.
- What still works: pages that answer real questions · consistent name, address and phone everywhere · real reviews · fast, mobile-friendly pages
- Sakthi · Founder, Astra Nexora

```
"SEO is dead. Everyone uses ChatGPT now."

I hear this every week. Here's the problem with it:

AI doesn't invent its answers.
It builds them from websites, reviews, directories and Google listings.

So when AI recommends a business, it's picking the one that's:
→ Described clearly on its own website
→ Listed with the same name, address and phone everywhere
→ Backed by real, recent reviews
→ Answering the questions customers actually ask

That's SEO.

What's dying is the old version: keyword stuffing, thin pages and buying links.

What matters now is being the clearest, most trusted answer, for Google and for AI.

Agree or disagree? 👇

#SEO #AISearch #DigitalMarketing
