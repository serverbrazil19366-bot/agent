## Tue 3 Nov: "They search for the price"

Poster: `posters/2026-11-03.png`

```
Patients don't search for "the best dentist in Chennai".
They search for the price.

On a dental clinic website I work on, the pages that pulled the most visitors in one week weren't the homepage. They were:

→ Tooth Implant Cost in Chennai
→ Invisible Aligners Cost in Chennai
→ Book Dental Appointment Chennai

People decide with a number in mind. They want to know if a treatment is within reach before they call.

If your website says "call for details", you're invisible at the exact moment a patient is ready to choose.

What I'd do on any clinic website:
1. One page for each high-value treatment
2. A clear price range, or what changes the price
3. The questions patients actually ask, answered on the page
4. A WhatsApp / call button they can tap without scrolling

Clinic owners: do you show treatment prices on your website? Why, or why not?

#DentalMarketing #LocalSEO #HealthcareMarketing
```

## Mon 9 Nov: myth vs fact

Poster: split card (to make). Left, red: "MYTH: A new website will bring more customers." Right, green: "FACT: A website brings customers when people can find it, trust it and contact you in one tap."

```
"We need a new website."

It's the most common thing business owners tell me. And it's often the wrong fix.

A dental lab I worked with asked for a new website. When I looked closer, the real problems were elsewhere: cases arriving on WhatsApp, scan files lost in chats, and Google not showing them for the searches that mattered.

The website was 10% of the work. The system around it changed the business.

Before you redesign, check 3 things:
1. Can people find you on Google for what you sell, in your area?
2. Does the page answer their first question in 5 seconds?
3. Can they call or WhatsApp you in one tap?

If those three fail, a prettier design won't fix it.

#WebDesign #SmallBusiness #DigitalMarketing
```
