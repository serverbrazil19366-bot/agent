1. Tue 10 Nov · Wix vs WordPress vs custom code
==================================================

"Should I build my website on Wix, WordPress, or get it custom coded?"

I get asked this a lot. There's no single best answer, but there is a right answer for your business.

Wix
✓ Easiest to start, drag and drop
✓ Hosting included in the monthly plan
✗ Less control, harder to grow with complex needs

WordPress
✓ Very flexible, plugins for almost everything
✓ Huge community, easy to find help
✗ Needs hosting, updates and security care

Custom code
✓ Fastest and fully yours
✓ Built exactly around your business
✗ You need a developer for most changes

The question that decides it:
Who will update the site, and how often?

Your team will edit it themselves → pick something easy, like Wix or WordPress
A few updates a month → WordPress works well
You need custom features built around your business → custom code

Which one is your website on right now?

2. Wed 11 Nov · 3 ways to reply to Google reviews
==================================================

Most businesses reply "Thank you!" to every Google review.
That's a missed chance.

People reading your reviews are deciding whether to choose you. Your replies show them how you treat customers.

Here are 3 simple templates:

★★★★★ Good review
"Thank you, [Name]! So glad the [service] went well. See you next time."
→ Use their name and mention the service.

★★★ Average review
"Thanks for the honest feedback, [Name]. We're working on [issue]. Hope to serve you better on your next visit."
→ Acknowledge the problem. Show you're fixing it.

★ Bad review
"We're sorry about this, [Name]. Please call us on [number] so we can make it right."
→ Stay calm. Take the conversation offline.

And one rule: reply within 48 hours, every time.

Do you reply to your Google reviews?

6. Sun 15 Nov · 8 marketing terms in plain words (carousel)
Carousel: upload 2026-11-15-sakthi-carousel.pdf as a Document
==================================================

Your agency's report says CTR, CPC, CPL and GBP.
Here's what they actually mean, in plain words. 👇

SEO: showing up on Google without paying for each click
Local SEO: showing up for "near me" searches and on Google Maps
GBP: your Google Business Profile, the listing on Search and Maps
CTR: of everyone who saw you, how many clicked
CPC: what you pay for each ad click
CPL: what you pay for one enquiry
Conversion rate: of every 100 visitors, how many call, book or buy
Retargeting: ads shown to people who already visited your site

If you only track one: CPL. Clicks are cheap. Enquiries pay the bills.

8. Tue 17 Nov · 5 things that make a website slow
==================================================

"My website is slow because of the internet."

Usually, it isn't. It's one of these 5 things:

1. Huge images
Photos uploaded straight from a phone can be several MB each.
Fix: resize and compress them before uploading (WebP works well).

2. Too many plugins and scripts
Chat widgets, trackers, pop-ups, sliders. Each one adds weight.
Fix: remove anything you don't actively use.

3. Cheap, slow hosting
The cheapest plan is often shared by hundreds of sites.
Fix: pick hosting based on speed, not just price.

4. No caching
The server builds every page from scratch for every visitor.
Fix: turn on caching (most hosts and WordPress plugins offer it).

5. Auto-playing videos and heavy sliders
They look nice and slow everything down, especially on mobile.
Fix: use one strong image instead.

Check your site for free on Google PageSpeed Insights. Test the mobile score.

What's your website's mobile score?

Before any website goes live, it has to pass these 10 checks.

2. Test the speed on PageSpeed Insights, mobile score first.
3. Submit every form yourself. Check the enquiry actually arrives (and the spam folder).

5. Padlock (https) on every page.
6. Every page has its own title and description for Google.
7. Google Analytics and Search Console set up before launch.
8. Sitemap submitted in Search Console.
9. Every link clicked. No broken pages, no leftover "Lorem ipsum".
10. Name, address and phone match your Google Business Profile.

The one that's skipped most often: number 3.
A form that looks fine but never sends the enquiry is worse than no form at all.

A big discount

20. Sun 29 Nov · Myth: Google Ads will fix a slow month
==================================================

"It's a slow month. Let's run Google Ads."

A slow month isn't always an ads problem.

Ads bring more people to your door. They can't fix what happens after they arrive.
More visitors ≠ more sales.

Before you spend more on ads, check these 3:

1. Are enquiries answered within an hour?
A missed call or a late WhatsApp reply is a lost customer, ad or no ad.

2. Does your landing page say what you do, where, and the price range?
If people have to call to find out the basics, many won't.

3. Do you know which calls and enquiries came from ads?
Without tracking, you can't tell if the money worked.

If all 3 are fine, ads can help.
If not, more ads only mean more water poured into a leaking bucket.

Fix the leak first. Then turn up the tap.

What do you usually do in a slow month?
