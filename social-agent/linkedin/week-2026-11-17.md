## Tue 17 Nov: 5 things that make a website slow

Poster text:
- WEBSITE SPEED
- Your website isn't slow because of your internet.
- **It's these 5 things.**
- 1. Huge images: photos uploaded straight from the phone
- 2. Too many plugins and scripts: chat widgets, trackers, pop-ups
- 3. Cheap, slow hosting
- 4. No caching
- 5. Auto-playing videos and heavy sliders
- Slow site = visitors leave before they see your offer.

```
"My website is slow because of the internet."

Usually, it isn't. It's one of these 5 things:

1. Huge images
Photos uploaded straight from a phone can be several MB each.
Fix: resize and compress them before uploading (WebP works well).

2. Too many plugins and scripts
Chat widgets, trackers, pop-ups, sliders. Each one adds weight.
Fix: remove anything you don't actively use.

3. Cheap, slow hosting
The cheapest plan is often shared by hundreds of sites.
Fix: pick hosting based on speed, not just price.

4. No caching
The server builds every page from scratch for every visitor.
Fix: turn on caching (most hosts and WordPress plugins offer it).

5. Auto-playing videos and heavy sliders
They look nice and slow everything down, especially on mobile.
Fix: use one strong image instead.

Check your site for free on Google PageSpeed Insights. Test the mobile score.

What's your website's mobile score?

#WebsiteSpeed #WebDesign #SmallBusiness
```

## Sat 21 Nov: 5 design rules for a post people stop at

Poster text:
- DESIGN RULES
- Most business posts fail **in the first second.**
- 1. One message per post
- 2. Headline readable on a phone in 3 seconds
- 3. One clear action
- 4. The same brand colours every time
- 5. Real photos over stock photos
- If it needs explaining, it needs redesigning.

```
Most business posts fail in the first second.

Not because the offer is bad. Because nobody could tell what it was.

5 rules I follow for every post:

1. One message per post
Not your full service list. One idea.

2. Readable on a phone in 3 seconds
Most people see your post on a small screen, while scrolling.

3. One clear action
Call, WhatsApp, visit, book. Pick one.

4. The same brand colours every time
People should recognise you before they read your name.

5. Real photos over stock
Your shop, your team, your work. People trust what looks real.

If a post needs explaining, it needs redesigning.

Which rule do you break most often?
```

```
Every local business needs to show up for 3 kinds of search:

1. "[service] near me"
People who want you now, nearby.
→ Comes mostly from your Google Business Profile: right category, address, hours and reviews.

2. "[service] in [your area]"
People who know where they want to go.
→ Needs a page on your website for each area you serve.

3. "[service] cost in [your city]"
People comparing before they decide.
→ Needs a page that talks honestly about price, or what changes it.

Most businesses only try for the first one.
The other two are often easier to win.
```
