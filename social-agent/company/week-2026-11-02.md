Poster text:
- WEBSITE CHECK
- Visitors decide in seconds.
- **Your first screen must answer 5 questions.**
- 1. What do you do?
- 2. Where do you do it?
- 3. Who is it for?
- 4. Why should I trust you?
- 5. How do I contact you? (Call / WhatsApp button)
- If they have to scroll to find these, many won't.
- @astranexora

Instagram:
```
Visitors decide in seconds whether to stay on your website ⏱️

1️⃣ What do you do?
"Dental clinic", "interior design", "home bakery". Say it plainly.

Your city or area. Local customers look for this first.

## Thu 5 Nov: Myth, "more followers = more customers"

Poster text:
- MYTH vs FACT
- MYTH: **"More followers = more customers."**
- FACT: Customers come from the right people seeing a clear offer, with an easy way to contact you.
- What matters more than followers:
- ✔ Followers who live near you and need what you sell
- ✔ Posts that say what you offer and the price range
- ✔ Enquiries on WhatsApp and calls
- Followers don't pay the bills. Enquiries do.
- @astranexora

Instagram:
```
MYTH: More followers = more customers ❌

FACT: Customers come from the right people seeing a clear offer, with an easy way to contact you ✅

✔️ Followers who are near you and need what you sell

## Fri 6 Nov: Set a Deepavali away message on WhatsApp Business

Poster text:
- WHATSAPP AUTOMATION
- Closed for Deepavali?
- **Don't leave customers on "seen".**
- Set an away message in 2 minutes:
- WhatsApp Business → Settings → Business tools → Away message
- Choose "Custom schedule" and set your holiday dates
- Example: "Happy Deepavali! 🪔 We're closed from [date] to [date]. We'll reply on [date]."
- Customers get an instant reply. You enjoy the festival.
- @astranexora

Instagram:
```

## Sat 7 Nov: Update your Google Business Profile with festival hours

Poster text:
- LOCAL TIP
- Customers check Google before they visit.
- **Are your Deepavali hours right?**
- Add "special hours" on your Google Business Profile:
- 1. Open your profile on Google Search or Maps
- 2. Edit profile → Hours → Special hours
- 3. Add your festival dates and timings (or "Closed")
- No wasted trips. No angry reviews.
- @astranexora

Instagram:
```
Deepavali is tomorrow 🪔 Customers will check Google before they visit you.
