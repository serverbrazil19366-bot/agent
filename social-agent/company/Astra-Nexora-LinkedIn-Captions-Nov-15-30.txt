MON 16 NOV · Get more Google reviews
============================================================

Happy customers rarely leave reviews.
Not because they don't like you. Because nobody asked.

Reviews help you rank higher on Google Maps, and they're the first thing new customers read.

4 simple ways to get more:

1. Ask on the same day
Right after the service, while they're still happy.

2. Send your direct review link on WhatsApp
Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews" gives you a short link.

3. Put a review QR code at the counter
Right next to your payment QR.

4. Reply to every review
People leave reviews where they see the owner reads them.

One rule: never buy reviews or offer gifts for them. Google can remove them.

MON 30 NOV · 5 Search Console checks every month
============================================================

15 minutes a month in Google Search Console tells you what's actually working on your website.

Check these 5 on the 1st of every month:

1. Total clicks
Up or down compared with last month?

2. Top search queries
What people really type to find you. Often a surprise.

3. Pages with impressions but few clicks
People see them on Google but don't click. Improve the title and description.

4. Page indexing
Are any important pages missing from Google?

5. Core Web Vitals
Speed and mobile issues that hold your rankings back.

No Search Console set up yet? That's step zero.
