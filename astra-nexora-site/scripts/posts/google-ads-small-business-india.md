Google Ads lets your business appear at the top of Google when someone searches for exactly what you offer. For a small business, it's one of the fastest ways to get calls and enquiries. It's also one of the fastest ways to waste money if it's set up badly. Here's how to do it properly.

## How Google Ads works

You choose the searches (keywords) you want to appear for, write ads, set a budget and pay when someone clicks. Google decides which ads to show based on your bid and how relevant and useful your ad and landing page are.

## Choose the right campaign type

- **Search campaigns:** text ads on Google search results. Best for most small businesses because people are actively looking.
- **Performance Max:** runs across Google's network automatically. Can work well once you have conversion tracking and data.
- **Local and call-focused ads:** show your phone number and location, ideal for service businesses.
- **Display and YouTube:** good for awareness and remarketing, less for immediate enquiries.

![Reviewing ad campaign performance](pexels-577210 "Start with search campaigns for the services that bring the most enquiries.")

## Pick keywords that show buying intent

Focus on searches that show someone wants to buy or book: "dentist near me", "website design company", "AC repair service". Avoid broad terms like "what is SEO" that bring curious visitors who won't contact you.

Add **negative keywords** to stop your ads showing for searches like "free", "jobs", "course" or "salary" if they don't apply to you.

## Set a sensible budget

Start with a daily budget you can sustain for at least a month, so Google has enough data to optimise. Judge results on cost per enquiry, not cost per click.

## Target the right locations

Show your ads only where your customers are. For a local business, that might be a radius around your shop or the districts you serve.

## Your landing page matters

The page people land on should match the ad, load fast on mobile and make it easy to call, WhatsApp or fill a short form. A great ad sent to a slow, confusing page wastes money.

![A customer contacting a business on a smartphone](pexels-5721014 "Make calling or messaging you one tap away.")

## Track what matters

Set up conversion tracking for calls, form submissions and WhatsApp clicks. Without it, you can't tell which keywords bring customers and which only spend money.

## Common mistakes that waste money

1. Using broad match keywords without negative keywords.
2. Sending all ads to the home page.
3. No conversion tracking.
4. Running ads 24/7 when nobody answers the phone.
5. Setting up a campaign and never checking the search terms report.

## Frequently asked questions

Q: How much does Google Ads cost in India?
A: You set your own budget and pay per click. Costs per click vary a lot by industry and competition, so start with a budget you can sustain for a month and measure cost per enquiry.
Q: How quickly can Google Ads bring enquiries?
A: Often within the first few days of launch, once the campaign is approved and running.
Q: Can I run Google Ads myself?
A: Yes, but it's easy to waste budget without the right targeting and tracking. An experienced manager usually pays for themselves by cutting waste.
Q: Do I need a website to run Google Ads?
A: You can run call-only ads without one, but a fast, clear landing page usually brings much better results.
