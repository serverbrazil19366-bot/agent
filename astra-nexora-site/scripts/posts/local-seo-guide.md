When someone searches "dentist near me", "interior designer in Coimbatore" or "AC service Madurai", Google shows a map with three businesses and a list of websites underneath. Local SEO is the work that decides whether your business is in that list.

For most small businesses this is the most valuable kind of SEO there is. The people searching are nearby, they know what they want, and many of them are ready to call today. Here is how to approach it, step by step.

## How local search works

Google decides local results on three broad factors, and it says so in its own help pages:

- **Relevance:** how well your business matches what was searched.
- **Distance:** how far you are from the searcher or the place they named.
- **Prominence:** how well known and trusted your business is, from reviews, links and mentions across the web.

You cannot change distance, but you can work on relevance and prominence. Everything below does one or both.

## Step by step

### 1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile

This is the listing that appears in Maps and in the map box on Google. Choose the most specific primary category, add your services, hours, phone, website and real photos, and keep it updated. We cover this in detail in our [Google Business Profile guide](google-business-profile-guide.html).

### 2. Make your name, address and phone consistent

Your business name, address and phone number (often called NAP) should be written exactly the same way on your website, your Business Profile, social profiles and directories like Justdial or Sulekha. Mismatches confuse Google and customers.

### 3. Build pages for each service and area

One page that lists everything you do rarely ranks for anything. Give each main service its own page, and if you serve several towns, create genuinely useful pages for them with local details, not copies with the town name swapped.

### 4. Earn reviews, and reply to them

Ask every happy customer for a Google review, ideally with a direct link. Reply to every review, good or bad, politely and quickly. Never buy or fake reviews: it breaks Google's rules and can get your profile suspended.

### 5. Get listed where your customers look

List your business on the main directories and on industry sites relevant to you. Local newspapers, associations and suppliers can also mention or link to you. These signals add to your prominence.

### 6. Fix the website basics

Local visitors are mostly on phones. Your site must load fast on mobile data, show your phone and WhatsApp clearly, and have your address and service area on every page, usually in the footer.

## How long does local SEO take?

If your profile and website are in poor shape, fixing them can bring improvements within weeks. Ranking in a competitive area usually takes several months of steady work: content, reviews and mentions build up over time. Anyone who guarantees a top position is not being honest with you, because nobody controls Google's results.

## Frequently asked questions

Q: Do I need a website for local SEO, or is a Business Profile enough?
A: A Business Profile alone can bring calls, but a good website makes your profile stronger and lets you rank in normal results as well as Maps.
Q: Can I rank in a town where I don't have an office?
A: In Maps, rankings depend heavily on your address. In normal results, a helpful page about your service in that town can rank if you genuinely serve it.
Q: Is local SEO a one-time job?
A: The setup is a one-time job, but reviews, posts, photos and new pages need regular attention to keep and improve your position.
