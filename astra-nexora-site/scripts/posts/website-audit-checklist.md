Most businesses start marketing by spending on ads or posting more on social media. But every one of those visitors lands on your website. If the website is slow, confusing or hard to contact you from, you pay for the click and lose the customer.

Before you spend another rupee on traffic, run through these 12 checks. You don't need technical skills for most of them, just your phone, a laptop and twenty minutes.

## Speed and mobile

### 1. Does it load fast on mobile data?

Switch off WiFi and open your website on your phone using mobile data. If you are waiting for the page to appear, so are your customers, and many of them will leave. You can also test it free on [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev). Large uncompressed images are the most common cause, and also the easiest to fix.

### 2. Is it easy to use on a phone?

Most of your visitors will be on a phone. Check that the text is readable without zooming, buttons are big enough to tap, and nothing spills off the side of the screen.

### 3. Do all the links and forms actually work?

Click every menu link, every button and submit your own contact form. Broken links and forms that send nowhere are surprisingly common, and they quietly cost you enquiries.

## Getting found on Google

### 4. Does Google know your website exists?

Search Google for **site:yourwebsite.com**. If few or none of your pages appear, Google has not indexed them yet. Setting up [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) and submitting a sitemap is the first step.

### 5. Does each page have a clear title and description?

The title is the blue link people see in Google results. Each page should have its own title that says what the page is about and who it is for, not just your company name or "Home".

### 6. Is your Google Business Profile complete?

For local searches, your Business Profile often shows up before your website does. Make sure the category, hours, phone, website link and photos are filled in and match your website exactly.

## Trust and clarity

### 7. Can a visitor tell what you do in five seconds?

Open your home page and read only what is visible without scrolling. Is it clear what you offer, who it is for and what to do next? If the top of the page is a vague slogan, rewrite it in plain words.

### 8. Do you show real proof?

Real photos of your team and work, genuine client reviews (with permission) and clear details about who you are all build trust. Stock photos alone and claims without proof do the opposite.

### 9. Is it secure?

Your address bar should show a padlock (https). Browsers warn visitors about sites without it, and many people leave at that warning.

## Turning visitors into enquiries

### 10. Is there one clear next step on every page?

Every page should end with an obvious action: call, WhatsApp, book or request a quote. Don't make people hunt for your contact page.

### 11. Is contacting you effortless?

For many businesses in India, a WhatsApp button gets more enquiries than a long form. Keep forms short and ask only for what you need to reply.

### 12. Are you tracking enquiries?

If you don't know which pages or channels bring in calls and messages, you can't tell what is working. Set up Google Analytics with conversion tracking before you start any paid campaign.

## What to fix first

Start with whatever stops people from contacting you: broken forms, slow mobile loading and an unclear first screen. Then work on Google visibility. Ads come last, because they only multiply what your website already does, good or bad.
