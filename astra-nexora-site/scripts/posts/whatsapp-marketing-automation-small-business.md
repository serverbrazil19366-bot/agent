In India, WhatsApp is where your customers already are. They message friends, family and businesses every day. If contacting you means filling a long form or calling during busy hours, many will simply message a competitor instead. Here's how small businesses can use WhatsApp for marketing and automation, step by step.

## Start with the WhatsApp Business app

The free WhatsApp Business app gives you a business profile, catalogue, quick replies, labels and greeting and away messages. Set these up first: business hours, address, website and a short description of what you do.

## Make WhatsApp one tap away on your website

Add a WhatsApp button to every page, especially on mobile. Better still, use a short form (name, phone, service, message) that opens WhatsApp with the enquiry already written. The customer just presses send, and you receive every request in the same clear format.

We built exactly this for a dental clinic: patients fill four fields and WhatsApp opens with the appointment request ready to send.

## Use your catalogue and quick replies

Add your main products or services to the WhatsApp catalogue with prices or starting prices. Save quick replies for common questions: location, timings, pricing and how to book. You'll reply in seconds instead of typing the same answer again.

## When you need the WhatsApp Business API

The app works for one phone and manual replies. The **WhatsApp Business API** (through an approved provider) lets you:

- Send automatic booking confirmations and reminders
- Connect WhatsApp to your CRM, website and Google Sheets
- Build chatbots that answer common questions
- Let several team members reply from one number

Tools like n8n can connect the API to the rest of your systems, so a new booking automatically notifies your team, updates a sheet and confirms with the customer.

## Broadcasts and campaigns, done right

Only message customers who have agreed to hear from you, and keep messages useful: offers, reminders, new products or seasonal greetings. Too many messages and people block you, which hurts your account.

![Reviewing automated messages and bookings on a laptop](pexels-16094042 "Automation handles the repetitive replies so your team can focus on customers.")

## Ideas for different businesses

- **Clinics:** appointment booking, reminders and post-visit follow-ups.
- **Shops:** catalogue, order confirmations and delivery updates.
- **Service businesses:** quote requests, booking slots and job updates.
- **B2B:** order or case submissions with instant team notifications.

## Measure it

Track how many WhatsApp enquiries come from your website, ads and Google Business Profile. Add WhatsApp click tracking in Google Analytics so you can see which pages bring conversations.

## Frequently asked questions

Q: Is WhatsApp marketing free?
A: The WhatsApp Business app is free. The Business API charges per conversation through approved providers.
Q: Can WhatsApp replies be automated?
A: Yes. The app has greeting and away messages and quick replies; the Business API allows full automation and chatbots.
Q: Will customers find WhatsApp messages spammy?
A: Not if they opted in and the messages are useful. Send only what helps them: confirmations, reminders and relevant offers.
Q: Can WhatsApp connect to my website and CRM?
A: Yes. With the Business API and tools like n8n, WhatsApp can connect to forms, sheets, CRMs and dashboards.
