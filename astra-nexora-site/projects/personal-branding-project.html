Personal Branding Project

A personal brand plan for a business leader: positioning, LinkedIn presence and a steady content system.

  • Client typeFounder and business leader
  • ServicesPositioning, LinkedIn, Content, PR
  • Duration90-day plan
  • StatusConcept project

This is a concept project: it shows how we would approach this type of brief. It is not a client case study, and it contains no client results.

✦The brief

The challenge.

A respected founder was well known offline but almost invisible online. Clients, partners and hires searched for them and found an empty LinkedIn profile and no point of view.

✦Our approach

How we'd solve it.

  1. 01

    Find the voice

    Interviews to define expertise, opinions and the audience to reach.

  2. 02

    Fix the profile

    A rewritten LinkedIn profile, banner and featured section.

  3. 03

    Content system

    Weekly posts, short videos and articles drafted for approval.

  4. 04

    Visibility

    Podcast, event and media opportunities that match the positioning.

✦Scope

What we'd deliver.

Part of our Branding & Personal Branding service.

  • ✓Personal brand positioning
  • ✓LinkedIn profile rewrite and design
  • ✓90-day content calendar
  • ✓Post and video drafts
  • ✓Media and speaking opportunity list
✦Success measures

What success looks like.

The goals we'd set and report against for a project like this.

  • 01

    A complete, credible LinkedIn presence

  • 02

    Consistent weekly posting without the founder writing from scratch

  • 03

    A clear point of view people associate with the founder

  • 04

    Inbound conversations from the right audience
