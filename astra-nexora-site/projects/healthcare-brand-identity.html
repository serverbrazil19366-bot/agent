Healthcare Brand Identity

A brand identity system for a new healthcare provider that needs to earn patient trust from day one.

CONCEPT PROJECT
  • Client typeNew healthcare provider
  • ServicesBrand strategy, Identity, Guidelines
  • DeliverableComplete identity system
  • StatusConcept project

✦This is a concept project. It shows how we would approach this type of brief. It is not a client case study and contains no client results.

The brief

The challenge.

A new provider entering a crowded market had no recognition and needed patients to feel safe choosing them over established names, with a brand that works across signage, reports, social and the website.

Our approach

How we'd solve it.

Four steps, each with a clear outcome before the next one starts.

  1. 01

    Patient research

    Understand what patients worry about and what makes them trust a provider.

  2. 02

    Positioning

    A clear promise and tone of voice: calm, expert and human.

  3. 03

    Identity design

    Logo, colour and type chosen for clarity and warmth, tested at every size.

  4. 04

    System & guidelines

    Templates and rules so every touchpoint looks consistent.

Scope

What we'd deliver.

Part of our Branding & Personal Branding →

  • ✓Brand positioning and messaging
  • ✓Logo suite and favicon
  • ✓Colour, typography and imagery system
  • ✓Brand guidelines document
  • ✓Stationery, signage and social templates
Success measures

What success looks like.

The goals we'd set and report against for a project like this.

  • 01

    Look established from the first day

  • 02

    Be recognisable at a glance across all touchpoints

  • 03

    Give staff and partners clear brand rules

  • 04

    Make the website and social launch-ready
