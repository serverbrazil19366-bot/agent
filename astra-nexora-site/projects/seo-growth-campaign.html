SEO Growth Campaign

A search strategy covering technical fixes, content and link building to lift organic visibility.

Concept project. It shows how we would approach this kind of brief. It is not a client case study and has no client results.

Client type
Local service business
Services
Technical SEO, Content, Links, Analytics
Duration
6-month plan
Status
Concept project
(01)The brief

The challenge.

The business had a website but almost no organic traffic. Technical errors blocked indexing, service pages were thin, and competitors dominated the map pack for every important search.

(02)Approach

How we'd solve it.

Four steps, each with a clear outcome before the next one starts.

  1. 01

    Fix the foundations

    Crawl errors, speed, indexing and schema fixed in the first month.

  2. 02

    Own the services

    One in-depth page per service, built around real search terms.

  3. 03

    Win local

    Google Business Profile optimisation, local listings and a review plan.

  4. 04

    Earn authority

    Relevant links and mentions from local and industry websites.

(03)Scope

What we'd deliver.

  • Technical SEO audit and fixes
  • Keyword map and content plan
  • New service and location pages
  • Google Business Profile optimisation
  • Monthly SEO dashboard
Part of Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
(04)Success measures

What success looks like.

The goals we'd set and report against for a project like this.

  1. 01

    Fix every critical technical issue in month one

  2. 02

    Rank on page one for priority service keywords

  3. 03

    Appear in the local map pack for core searches

  4. 04

    Grow enquiries from organic search month on month
