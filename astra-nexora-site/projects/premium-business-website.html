Premium Business Website

A complete website concept for a financial services firm, taking an outdated site to a modern, credible online presence.

  • Client typeFinancial services firm
  • ServicesStrategy, UI/UX, Development, SEO
  • PlatformWordPress
  • StatusConcept project

This is a concept project: it shows how we would approach this type of brief. It is not a client case study, and it contains no client results.

✦The brief

The challenge.

The firm's site looked dated, loaded slowly on phones and didn't explain its services clearly. Prospective clients couldn't tell why they should trust the firm with their money, and the contact form was buried.

✦Our approach

How we'd solve it.

  1. 01

    Positioning first

    Define the firm's audiences and the three reasons they should choose this firm, before any design.

  2. 02

    Clear structure

    A simple sitemap with one page per service, plus pages that build trust: team, credentials and process.

  3. 03

    Premium, calm design

    A restrained palette, strong typography and real photography to signal stability.

  4. 04

    Built for enquiries

    Visible calls to action, short forms, WhatsApp and tracking on every enquiry.

✦Scope

What we'd deliver.

Part of our Website Design & Development service.

  • ✓Sitemap and wireframes
  • ✓Desktop and mobile page designs
  • ✓WordPress build with editable blocks
  • ✓On-page SEO and schema
  • ✓Analytics and conversion tracking
✦Success measures

What success looks like.

The goals we'd set and report against for a project like this.

  • 01

    Load in under 2.5 seconds on mobile

  • 02

    Make every service understandable in one screen

  • 03

    Give every page one clear next step

  • 04

    Track every enquiry back to its source
