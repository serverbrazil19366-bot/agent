What is n8n?

n8n is a workflow automation tool that connects apps and moves data between them, similar to Zapier but more flexible and often cheaper at scale.

Will a chatbot replace my team?

No. It handles common questions and captures details instantly, then hands real conversations to your team.

Is my customer data safe?

We design workflows with access limited to what's needed and keep data in tools your business owns.
