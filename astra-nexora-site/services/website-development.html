WordPress, Wix or custom: which should I choose?

WordPress suits most growing businesses, Wix suits simple sites you want to edit yourself, and custom code suits special requirements. We recommend one after understanding your needs.

Will I be able to edit the website myself?

Yes. We hand over a site you can update, with a short training session.

Do you provide hosting and domain?

We can help you choose and set up hosting and a domain in your name, so you always own them.
