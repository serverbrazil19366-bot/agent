LeadReach – Lead Generation SaaS

A multi-tenant SaaS I built solo that finds local businesses on Google Maps, reaches them on WhatsApp and email from each client's own number, and drops every reply into one inbox.

  • TypeSaaS product (self-built)

Try LeadReachTalk to me about it
  • The problem

    Local businesses need a steady flow of new customers, but searching for prospects, messaging each one and tracking replies takes hours they don't have.

  • The solution

    LeadReach, a multi-tenant SaaS that finds high-rated local businesses on Google Maps, reaches them on WhatsApp and email from the client's own number, and drops every reply into one inbox.

  • The result

    A complete product I architected and shipped solo: lead engine, multi-channel outreach, client dashboards, pay-first billing and plan-based limits, live with three monthly plans.

01The challenge

Finding new customers is the hardest job for a small business. Doing outreach properly means finding the right businesses in the right area, checking which ones are worth contacting, sending each a personal message, following up and keeping track of who replied. Most owners never get to it.

The tools that exist are either built for large sales teams, need a lot of setup, or send messages from someone else's number, so replies don't come back to the business. I wanted something a clinic owner could start by simply sharing their WhatsApp number.

02My approach

Build it as a done-for-you SaaS, not a do-it-yourself tool. The client tells us the industry and cities; LeadReach finds the leads every day, sends personalised messages from the client's own WhatsApp number and brand, and puts every reply in one inbox. The client only replies and closes.

Because many clients share one platform, every account is fully separated: its own data, its own WhatsApp and email credentials (encrypted), and limits that follow the plan it pays for.

03 What I built

The solution, step by step

01

Smart lead finding

LeadReach pulls fresh, high-rated local businesses from Google Maps through the Places API for the client's industry and cities, ranks them by quality and adds new ones every day, so outreach always starts with relevant businesses, not an old bought list.

02

WhatsApp outreach from the client's own number

Personalised template messages go out through the WhatsApp Cloud API from each client's own verified number and templates, so the brand is theirs and replies go straight to them.

03

Email fallback

Leads without WhatsApp are reached by email automatically, using each client's own SMTP settings.

04

Unified reply inbox and pipeline

Every reply lands in one inbox with delivery and read tracking. Each lead moves from New to Contacted to Converted, and the dashboard shows the whole pipeline.

05

Multi-tenant and secure

Every query is scoped to the client's account, so no client can see another's data. API keys and credentials are encrypted, and the platform runs on a hardened Linux server with SSL.

06

Daily send scheduler with plan caps

A scheduler sends each day's outreach automatically and stops at the plan's daily limit, which keeps volumes safe for the client's number.

07

A landing page that sells

The public site explains the product in three steps, shows a live preview of the workflow, answers common questions and lists what's included in every plan.

08

Pay-first signup and billing

Three monthly plans (Starter ₹3,499, Growth ₹6,999 and Pro ₹13,999) with features and daily lead limits unlocked by plan. New clients pay by UPI first, then the account is set up and the login sent. Admin and billing dashboards manage every account.

04 The result

What changed

  • Built end to end by one person, from the lead-finding engine to billing and client dashboards
  • Live with a public landing page, pay-first UPI signup and three monthly plans
  • Two outreach channels (WhatsApp and email) with replies, delivery and read status in one inbox
  • Multi-tenant from day one: separate, encrypted data and credentials for every client
