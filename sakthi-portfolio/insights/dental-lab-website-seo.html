Dental lab website & SEO: Ankhor Dental Lab

By S. Saktheeswaran, founder of Astra Nexora · 7 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

A dental lab's customers are dentists, and they look for very specific things: zirconia, implant prostheses, dentures, digital workflows. I built the Ankhor Dental Lab website and did its SEO so dentists can find the right product, trust the lab and send a case in a few clicks.

What the website needed to do

Explain what the lab makes and how it works, show the technology behind it, and make sending a case the obvious next step from every page.

A product catalogue with a page for every product

The products section works like a catalogue: categories such as fixed, implant, removable, digital and specialised, with search and a page for each product. Dentists can go straight to zirconia or a specific implant prosthesis instead of reading one long list.

Technology and services pages

A technology page shows the lab's fully digital workflow, from scanning and CAD/CAM design to milling, printing and finishing, and the services pages explain what the lab offers. These pages build trust with dentists comparing labs.

SEO for every page

Every page has its own title and description, clear headings and internal links between related products, and the site has a sitemap so Google can find all the product pages. Because each product has its own page, each one can show up for its own searches.

Send a Case everywhere

A Send a Case button sits in the header on every page, so a dentist who finds the right product can start a case immediately. The case portal behind that button is a separate system, which I've written about on its own.

Quick checklist

  • Product catalogue with a page per product
  • Categories and search for products
  • Technology and services pages that build trust
  • Unique titles and descriptions
  • Sitemap covering every product page
  • Send a Case on every page
