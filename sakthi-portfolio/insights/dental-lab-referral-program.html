Diwali referral program for Ankhor Dental Lab

By S. Saktheeswaran, founder of Astra Nexora · 7 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

Dentists trust other dentists' recommendations more than any ad. For Diwali, Ankhor Dental Lab wanted to turn that into new clinics, so I built a Refer & Earn program: a campaign page, a referral dashboard and an app to track it all.

The offer

Refer three doctors and earn two free premium zirconia crowns, worth ₹5,000, on cases booked before the offer ends. Referred dentists get a welcome offer on their first case, and there's a surprise reward for the first referral.

A campaign page built to convert

The Refer & Earn page leads with the reward, shows a live countdown to the end of the offer, explains how it works in three simple steps and answers common questions. A Get My Referral Link button sits right at the top.

Personal referral links, shared on WhatsApp

Each dentist gets a personal referral link and can share it with friends on WhatsApp in one tap, which is how dentists already talk to each other.

A dashboard and app to track referrals

Every referral is tracked in a dashboard and app, so the lab can see who referred whom, which referred dentists have sent their first case and who has earned a reward, without spreadsheets.

What any business can copy

Make the reward clear and worth it, add a real deadline, make sharing one tap on the channel people already use, and track every referral automatically so rewards are fair and on time.

Quick checklist

  • A clear reward worth sharing
  • A real deadline with a countdown
  • Three simple steps
  • Personal referral links
  • One-tap WhatsApp sharing
  • Dashboard and app to track referrals
