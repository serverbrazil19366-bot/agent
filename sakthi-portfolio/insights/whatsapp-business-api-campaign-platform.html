WhatsApp Business API campaigns for ProPedoZR

By S. Saktheeswaran, founder of Astra Nexora · 13 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

Most businesses that want to send WhatsApp campaigns sign up for a third-party platform and pay every month, plus a markup on every message. For ProPedoZR, a pediatric zirconia crown brand reaching 280+ dentists, I built the platform in-house instead, on the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Here's how it works and what I learned.

Why build instead of buy

Third-party WhatsApp tools cost ₹1,500–2,800 a month plus a per-message markup, and lock you into a dashboard you mostly don't use. ProPedoZR needed one thing done well: personalised offers to a known list of dentists, with replies going to a person. That's a small, focused system, and building it removed the subscription completely.

Getting onto the Meta Cloud API

The first step was Meta Business Verification with the company's registration details, then registering a dedicated sender number, adding billing and getting the display name approved. An early policy error turned out to be a trust gate for new accounts, and full business verification solved it.

Personalised, approved templates

Business-initiated WhatsApp messages must use a Meta-approved template. The template opens with Hello Dr. and a variable that the system fills with each doctor's name, then the crown offer and a Chat on WhatsApp button. Every doctor gets a message addressed to them, not a generic blast.

Sending safely from a new number

New numbers are fragile. The campaign runner sends in batches of 20 with a 300 ms gap, and the first day was capped at 150 messages, under the new-number daily limit, to build the sender's quality rating before scaling.

Cleaning the contact list first

The doctor list came from PDFs, Word files and raw exports. A pipeline parsed them, normalised every number, removed duplicates and stripped non-doctor contacts like the team's own numbers, patients, vendors and landlines. Sending to the wrong people is the fastest way to get reported and banned.

Replies that reach a person

Meta doesn't allow direct wa.me links in template buttons, so the button points to a redirect page on the company's own domain, which forwards to the team's staffed WhatsApp number. A webhook also records delivery receipts, read receipts and replies in the dashboard.

Results and what's next

On day 1, 150 doctors received a personalised message with 0 failures; the remaining 130 were scheduled for the next day. A full 280-doctor campaign costs roughly ₹250–300 in Meta fees. Next: automatic referral codes on reply, a reply inbox inside the dashboard, automatic throttling based on quality rating, and campaigns segmented by region.

Quick checklist

  • Complete Meta Business Verification first
  • Use an approved template with a name variable
  • Clean and de-duplicate contacts before sending
  • Send in small batches on a new number
  • Route replies to a human-staffed number
  • Track delivery, reads and replies with a webhook
  • Keep secrets off the web server's public paths

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a third-party provider to use the WhatsApp Business API?+

No. Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API can be used directly once your business is verified; you pay Meta's per-message fees.

How much does a WhatsApp campaign cost this way?+

Only Meta's message fees, about ₹1 per marketing message in this case, so a 280-contact campaign cost roughly ₹250–300.

How do you avoid getting the number banned?+

Use approved templates, message only relevant contacts, start with small daily volumes and batches, and make it easy to opt out.
