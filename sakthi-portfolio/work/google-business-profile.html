Google Business Profile Optimisation

Optimising Business Profiles so local customers find accurate details and trust the business.

  • Year2026
  • IndustryRestaurants & local businesses
  • ClientLocal businesses
  • RoleLocal SEO
Start a project
  • The problem

    Incomplete business profiles were costing local visibility on Google Search and Maps.

  • The solution

    Complete, accurate profiles with the right categories, services, photos and regular updates.

  • The result

    Optimised listings that give nearby customers the right details and a reason to choose the business.

01The challenge

For local businesses, the Google Business Profile is often the first thing a customer sees, before the website. Missing hours, wrong categories, few photos and inconsistent details make a business look less trustworthy and rank lower on Maps.

02My approach

Audit each profile, fix every detail, choose the right primary and secondary categories, add every service, add real photos, and keep the profile active with updates. Then track calls, direction requests and website clicks from the profile's insights.
