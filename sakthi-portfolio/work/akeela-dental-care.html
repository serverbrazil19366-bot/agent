Akeela Dental Care – Website & SEO

A new dental clinic website with a page for every treatment, full SEO and WhatsApp appointment booking.

  • Year2026
  • IndustryDental clinic
  • ClientAkeela Dental Care
  • RoleWebsite, SEO & booking
Visit live siteStart a project
  • The problem

    A dental clinic needed to be found by patients searching for treatments, and to make booking easy.

  • The solution

    A new website built around treatments, with full SEO and booking, calls and WhatsApp on every page.

  • The result

    A complete clinic website where every treatment has its own page and every page leads to a booking.

01The challenge

Akeela Dental Care has served patients since 1994 and offers a wide range of treatments, from implants and root canals to aligners and cosmetic dentistry. Patients search for those treatments by name and area, and they decide within seconds whether a clinic looks trustworthy.

The clinic needed a website that shows up for those searches, explains each treatment clearly and makes it effortless to book, especially on a phone.

02My approach

Build the website around treatments, with one dedicated page for each, linked together through a treatments overview. Do the SEO properly from the start on every page. Put reviews front and centre. And make booking, calls and WhatsApp reachable from every page, designed mobile first.

03 What I built

The solution, step by step

01

A page for every treatment

Dental implants, root canal treatment, aligners, orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry and more each have their own page explaining the problem, the treatment, what to expect and how to book.

02

A treatments hub

A treatments overview links every treatment page together, helping patients and Google understand the full range of care.

03

Full on-page SEO

Unique titles and descriptions with the treatment and area, one clear H1, structured headings, compressed images, internal links, structured data and Google Search Console.

04

Reviews and trust

A dedicated patient reviews page with real stories and ratings, alongside the clinic's history and experience.

05

Mobile first, booking everywhere

Call, WhatsApp and Book Appointment buttons are always within reach, with the booking flow built as its own system.

04 The result

What changed

  • A complete clinic website with a dedicated page for each treatment
  • SEO set up on every page from day one, with Search Console
  • Reviews and clinic credentials visible where patients decide
  • Booking, calls and WhatsApp one tap away on every page
