Ankhor Dental Lab – Website & SEO

A new dental lab website with a full product catalogue, technology pages, SEO on every page and Send a Case everywhere.

  • The problem

    A dental lab needed a website that shows everything it makes and makes sending a case easy.

  • The solution

    A new website with a full product catalogue, technology pages, SEO on every page and Send a Case everywhere.

  • The result

    A complete lab website where every product can be found on its own and every page leads to a case.

01The challenge

Ankhor Dental Lab's customers are dentists, and they look for very specific restorations: zirconia, implant prostheses, dentures, digital models. They compare labs on quality, technology and how easy they are to work with.

The lab needed a website that presents its full range clearly, proves its digital workflow and turns a visiting dentist into a case.

02My approach

Build the site like a catalogue, with categories, search and a page for every product. Explain the lab's fully digital workflow on a technology page. Do the SEO on every page so each product can show up in search. And put Send a Case in the header of every page.

03 What I built

The solution, step by step

01

Home page that sets the standard

A clear promise, the lab's history since 1994, key numbers and quick routes to products and Send a Case.

02

A product catalogue

Categories (fixed, implant, removable, digital, specialised), search and filters, so a dentist can go straight to what they need.

03

A page for every product

Each product, like metal-free zirconia, has its own page with the options, warranty and a call to action.

04

A technology page

Scanning, CAD/CAM design, milling, printing and finishing under one roof, shown with the lab's real machines and numbers.

05

SEO on every page

Unique titles and descriptions, clear headings, internal links between related products, image optimisation, a sitemap covering every product page and Search Console.

04 The result

What changed

  • A full product catalogue with a page for every product
  • Every page optimised for search, with a complete sitemap
  • The lab's digital workflow presented clearly to dentists
  • Send a Case available from every page
