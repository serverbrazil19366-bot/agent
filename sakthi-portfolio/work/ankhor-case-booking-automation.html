Ankhor Dental Lab – Case Portal, Scan Booking & Automation

An online case portal and scan booking platform with dashboards, email and WhatsApp alerts and a mobile app.

  • Year2026
  • IndustryDental laboratory
  • ClientAnkhor Dental Lab
  • RolePlanning, build & automation
Visit live siteStart a project
  • The problem

    Cases and scan bookings arrived through calls and scattered WhatsApp messages.

  • The solution

    An online case portal and a scan booking platform, each with a dashboard, plus instant email and WhatsApp alerts and a mobile app.

  • The result

    Every case and booking lands in one place, and the team hears about new cases instantly.

01The challenge

A dental lab runs on cases. Ankhor received them by phone and WhatsApp, details had to be copied by hand, and nobody could see every open case in one place. Scan visits were booked over the phone too, which meant back-and-forth and the risk of double bookings.

02My approach

Move both workflows online. Clinics send cases through one form that collects every detail and the scan files; the team is alerted instantly by email and WhatsApp and tracks everything in a dashboard and a mobile app. Scan visits are booked on a platform with live slots and their own dashboard.

03 What I built

The solution, step by step

01

Send a Case portal

Doctor and clinic details, case type and material, units, shade, a shade photo and STL, OBJ or PLY scan files, all in one form, so every case arrives complete.

02

Instant email and WhatsApp alerts

The moment a case is submitted, the lab gets an email and a WhatsApp notification with the details. Nobody has to keep checking inboxes or chats.

03

Case dashboard and mobile app

Every case lands in a dashboard where the team tracks it, and a mobile app lets them check cases away from the desk.

04

Scan booking with live slots

Clinics pick a free day and time and add their case details. Booked slots show as taken instantly, so there are no double bookings.

05

A clear process for clinics

The booking page explains the four steps, what to prepare and the pricing, and every booking goes into a dashboard for the team.

04 The result

What changed

  • Every case arrives complete, with scan files, in the same format
  • The team is notified by email and WhatsApp the moment a case comes in
  • All cases tracked in one dashboard and a mobile app
  • Scan visits booked online with no double bookings
