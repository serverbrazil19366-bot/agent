Our promises

Simple and honest. Questions? Message us on WhatsApp.

Shipping

  • We ship all over India.
  • Orders are packed and dispatched in 1–2 working days after we confirm your payment.
  • Delivery usually takes 3–7 days depending on your city.
  • Shipping is free above ₹499; below that a flat ₹60 is added.
  • Once shipped, you get the courier name and tracking number on WhatsApp and on the Track order page.
  • For festival and birthday gifts, please order at least 7 days before the date.

Replacements & cancellations

  • If an item arrives damaged or wrong, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours with an unboxing video, and we will send a replacement.
  • Hampers, chocolate bouquets and personalised items cannot be returned, because they are made to order.
  • You can cancel an order before it is shipped. If you have paid, we refund the full amount to the same UPI account within 3 working days.
  • Hair accessories and jewellery cannot be returned once used, for hygiene reasons.

Payments

  • We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm and others). Pay to the UPI ID shown at checkout only.
  • We never ask for your UPI PIN, OTP or card details. Never share them with anyone.
  • Every payment is checked by hand before we confirm your order.

Privacy

  • We collect your name, mobile number and address only to deliver your order and send order updates.
  • Your details are shared only with the courier company that delivers your parcel.
  • We never sell your details. To delete your details, message us on WhatsApp.
  • Your cart and saved address are stored only in your own browser.
